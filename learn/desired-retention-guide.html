How to choose desired retention如何选择目标记得率

Turning retention up feels like buying more memory. You are actually buying shorter intervals, and the bill arrives every day for months.把记得率往上调，感觉像买到更多记忆。你真正买到的是更短的间隔，而账单会在之后好几个月里每天送来。

What the setting actually means这个设置到底是什么意思

Desired retention is the recall probability at which a card is scheduled to come back. At 0.90 — Anki’s FSRS default — the scheduler aims for you to remember roughly nine of ten cards at the moment they are due. It is a target for the due moment, not a promise about your knowledge in general, and not a grade.目标记得率是"卡片被安排回来时的回忆概率"。设为 0.90——Anki 的 FSRS 默认值——排程器的目标是：在卡片到期的那一刻，你大约能记住十张里的九张。它是对到期时刻的目标，不是对你整体知识水平的承诺，也不是一个分数。

Why the cost curve bends upward代价曲线为什么会上翘

Because R decays as a power function of t/S, the time a card is allowed to rest shrinks sharply as the target approaches certainty. Moving 0.95 → 0.98 cuts intervals by a large factor across the whole deck, so daily reviews rise steeply while the number of extra cards you can recall rises slowly. The same deck, the same memory, a much bigger bill.由于 R 按 t/S 的幂函数衰减，当目标趋近"确定无疑"时，允许卡片休息的时间会急剧缩短。把 0.95 提到 0.98，会把整个牌组的间隔成倍压缩，于是每日复习量陡增，而多记住的卡片数增长缓慢。同样的牌组、同样的记忆，账单大得多。

Judge it per minute, not per cent按"每分钟"评判，而不是按百分比

The honest question is not "how much will I remember" but "how much will I remember per minute I spend". That ratio has a peak, and for many people the peak sits below 0.90. Recallo’s optimiser simulates each setting with your accuracy and your seconds-per-review, then divides recallable cards by daily minutes so you can see the peak rather than assume it.诚实的问题不是"我能记住多少"，而是"我每花一分钟能记住多少"。这个比值存在峰值，而对许多人来说，峰值落在 0.90 以下。Recallo 的优化器会用你的正确率与每卡秒数模拟每一档设置，再用可回忆卡数除以每日分钟，让你看见峰值，而不是假设它。

When paying more is correct什么时候值得多付

Efficiency is not the only objective. If forgetting one item has a serious consequence — a drug dose, a legal citation, a line you must deliver on a date — buy the higher target for that material specifically. The mistake is applying one global number to everything, which overpays on trivia and underpays where it matters.效率不是唯一目标。如果忘掉某一条会带来严重后果——药物剂量、法条引用、某个日子必须交付的台词——那就专门为这部分材料买更高的目标。真正的错误是给所有东西套一个全局数字：在无关紧要处付得过多，在要紧处付得不足。

The failure mode nobody models没人建模的那种失败

A high target produces a queue that grows for months. The realistic risk is not suboptimal scheduling; it is abandonment, and an abandoned deck retains nothing at any target. Pick a setting whose steady-state minutes per day you would still accept on a bad week — that constraint is more binding than any efficiency curve.高目标会带来一个持续增长数月的队列。真实的风险不是"排程不够优"，而是放弃；而被放弃的牌组，无论目标设多高，留存率都是零。挑一个"在糟糕的一周里你也仍然接受其稳态每日分钟数"的设置——这个约束比任何效率曲线都更有决定性。
