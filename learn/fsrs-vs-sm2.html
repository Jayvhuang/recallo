FSRS vs SM-2: what actually changedFSRS 与 SM-2：到底改了什么

Two schedulers, thirty years apart. The difference is not "newer is better" — it is which questions each one is able to ask.两个相隔三十年的排程器。差别不是"新的更好"，而是各自能问出什么问题。

SM-2 in one paragraph一段话讲完 SM-2

Published by Piotr Woźniak for SuperMemo, SM-2 stores one E-Factor per card, starting at 2.5 with a floor of 1.3. The first interval is 1 day, the second 6 days, and after that each interval is the previous one multiplied by the E-Factor. Grade well and the E-Factor drifts up; grade badly and it drifts down. Fail a card and the interval returns to the beginning.SM-2 由 Piotr Woźniak 为 SuperMemo 发表：每张卡存一个 E-Factor，初值 2.5，下界 1.3。第一个间隔 1 天，第二个 6 天，之后每个间隔等于上一个乘 E-Factor。评分好，E-Factor 上移；评分差，则下移。一旦答错，间隔回到起点。

What one number cannot separate一个数字分不开的两件事

The E-Factor has to carry two distinct facts at once: how intrinsically hard this card is, and how well you currently know it. Because they are fused, SM-2 cannot express "difficult card that is now very well learned" — so it tends to over-schedule mature cards and under-schedule fragile ones.E-Factor 必须同时承载两件不同的事实："这张卡本身有多难"和"你当前记得多牢"。由于二者被混在一起，SM-2 无法表达"很难但现在已经学得很牢的卡"——于是它倾向于把成熟卡排得过密，把脆弱卡排得过疏。

What FSRS addsFSRS 增加了什么

FSRS splits those apart into difficulty D and stability S, and adds retrievability R, computed from elapsed time. Because R is explicit, the next interval is solved for a retention target you choose rather than produced by a multiplier. That single change is what turns "how long should this wait" into a question with a stated objective.FSRS 把这两件事拆成难度 D 与稳定度 S，并加入由经过时间算出的可提取性 R。因为 R 是显式的，下一次间隔是按你选择的记得率目标解出来的，而不是由某个倍数生成的。正是这个改动，让"这张卡该等多久"成为一个有明确目标的问题。

The lapse rule is the biggest practical gap遗忘规则是最大的实际差距

SM-2 discards the interval on failure. FSRS computes post-lapse stability from what you had already built, so a card that survived four months does not restart like a card learned yesterday. In day-to-day use this is the difference people feel most: one bad session no longer wipes out a season of work.SM-2 在答错时丢弃间隔。FSRS 则用你已经建立的稳定度计算"遗忘后稳定度"，因此一张存活了四个月的卡不会像昨天刚学的卡那样重启。在日常使用中，这是人们感受最明显的差别：一次糟糕的复习不再抹掉一整季的成果。

Which should you use你该用哪个

If your app offers FSRS, it generally schedules more efficiently for the same target. But SM-2 is auditable on paper, has scheduled decks for decades, and is vastly better than no scheduling at all. The lowest-value move is switching algorithms while leaving badly written cards untouched.如果你的应用提供 FSRS，在相同目标下它通常排得更高效。但 SM-2 可以在纸上审计、已经为牌组服务了几十年，而且远胜于完全不排程。价值最低的动作，是换了算法却对写得糟糕的卡片不动一根手指。
