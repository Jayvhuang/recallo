Desired-retention optimiser目标记得率优化器

Raising desired retention feels like buying more memory. It is really buying shorter intervals — cost rises steeply while the extra cards you can recall rise slowly. This sweeps the whole range and reports the efficiency of each setting.把目标记得率往上调，感觉像"买到更多记忆"。实际上你买的是更短的间隔——代价陡增，而多记住的卡增长缓慢。这里扫过整个区间，报告每档设置的效率。

How this works它的原理

Retention is a price, not a quality setting记得率是价格，不是画质档位

Desired retention is the recall probability at which a card is scheduled to return. Because R decays as a power function of t/S, pushing the target near 1.0 forces intervals toward zero. The cost curve bends upward long before the benefit curve does.目标记得率是"卡片被安排回来时的回忆概率"。由于 R 按 t/S 的幂函数衰减，把目标推向 1.0 会把间隔压向零。代价曲线的上翘，远早于收益曲线。

Judge settings by cards per minute用"每分钟记住多少卡"来评判

The honest metric is not "how much do I remember" but "how much do I remember per minute I spend". Recallo simulates each setting and divides recallable cards by daily study minutes, so the efficiency peak is visible instead of assumed.诚实的指标不是"我记住了多少"，而是"我每花一分钟记住了多少"。Recallo 对每档设置做模拟，再用可回忆卡数除以每日学习分钟，让效率峰值可见，而不是靠假设。

The right answer depends on the stakes正确答案取决于代价结构

Efficiency argues for a lower target; consequences can argue for a higher one. If forgetting one item is catastrophic — a clinical dose, a legal citation — pay for a high target on that deck only. Splitting decks by stakes beats one global setting.效率倾向于更低的目标；后果可能要求更高的目标。如果忘掉一条的后果是灾难性的——临床剂量、法条引用——那就只对那一个牌组付高目标的代价。按"后果轻重"拆分牌组，胜过一个全局设置。

Questions常见问题

What is a good desired retention in FSRS?FSRS 的目标记得率设多少合适？

Anki’s FSRS default is 0.90, and it is a reasonable starting point rather than an optimum for everyone. Sweep the range here with your own accuracy and seconds-per-review: the setting with the best cards-per-minute is usually below 0.90, while deadlines and high-stakes material justify paying for more.Anki 的 FSRS 默认值是 0.90，它是个合理的起点，而不是对所有人最优。用你自己的正确率和每卡秒数在这里扫一遍：单看"每分钟记住多少卡"，最优往往低于 0.90；而临近考期或高风险材料，则值得为更高的目标付费。

Why does 98% retention cost so much more than 95%?为什么 98% 比 95% 贵那么多？

Because interval length is inversely related to how close the target sits to certainty. Going from 0.95 to 0.98 cuts the time each card is allowed to rest by a large factor, so the same deck generates far more reviews per day for a few extra remembered cards.因为目标越接近"确定无疑"，允许卡片休息的时间就越短。从 0.95 提到 0.98，会把每张卡的休息时间成倍砍掉，于是同一个牌组每天要产生多得多的复习，换来的只是多记住几张。

Does lowering retention mean I am learning worse?把记得率调低，是不是学得更差了？

It means more cards will fail on arrival — and a failed review is itself a strong learning event, provided you actually try to retrieve before flipping. The real risk of a low target is emotional: too many lapses feels like drowning, and an abandoned deck retains nothing.它意味着更多卡片在到期时会答错——而一次答错本身就是很强的学习事件，前提是你真的先尝试回忆再翻面。低目标真正的风险是情绪上的：失手太多会让人有溺水感，而被放弃的牌组，留存率是零。
