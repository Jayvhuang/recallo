Daily review workload simulator每日复习负荷模拟器

Twenty new cards a day sounds small. It is not twenty reviews a day — it compounds into a queue several times larger, and the queue keeps growing for months before it levels off. This simulates every card individually so you can pick a number you can keep.每天 20 张新卡听起来很少。但它不等于每天 20 次复习——它会累积成好几倍大的队列，而且要好几个月才趋于平稳。这里逐卡模拟，帮你选一个"能一直坚持"的数字。

How this works它的原理

Your queue is an accumulation, not a rate队列是累积量，不是速率

Each new card generates a whole future sequence of reviews. Add cards at a constant rate and today’s queue is the sum of every past card that happens to be due today. The steady state arrives only when the oldest cards’ intervals grow longer than the horizon you care about.每张新卡都会生成一整条未来的复习序列。以恒定速率加卡，今天的队列就是"所有过去的卡里恰好今天到期"的那些之和。只有当最老的卡的间隔超过你关心的时间跨度，负荷才真正进入稳态。

Simulated, not multiplied by a rule of thumb逐卡模拟，而非乘一个经验系数

Recallo runs each card through the same FSRS-5 state machine the scheduler uses, day by day, including lapses at the accuracy you specify. You get the queue curve and the steady-state figure, not a single fudge factor.Recallo 让每张卡都走一遍排程器所用的 FSRS-5 状态机，逐日推进，并按你设定的正确率注入遗忘。你得到的是队列曲线和稳态数值，而不是一个拍脑袋的系数。

Accuracy is the hidden lever正确率是隐藏的杠杆

Lapses are expensive twice: the failed review itself, and the shortened intervals that follow it. Drop your assumed accuracy by ten points and watch the steady-state load move — often more than changing the new-card count by the same proportion.遗忘的代价是双份的：一次失败的复习本身，加上之后被压短的一串间隔。把假定正确率下调十个百分点，看稳态负荷怎么动——通常比同比例改新卡数的影响更大。

Questions常见问题

How many new cards a day can most people sustain?大多数人每天能坚持多少张新卡？

There is no universal number, and anyone quoting one is guessing — it depends on your accuracy, your desired retention and how many seconds a review takes you. What is general is the shape: the load keeps climbing for months, so judge a new-card setting by its steady-state minutes per day, never by the first week.没有一个普适数字，报出具体数字的人都是在猜——它取决于你的正确率、目标记得率以及每次复习花几秒。真正普适的是形状：负荷会持续攀升数月，所以要用稳态的"每天多少分钟"来判断新卡设置，绝不能只看第一周。

Why does the curve keep rising for months?为什么曲线要涨好几个月？

Because intervals grow but never stop generating reviews. A card added today will come back this week, this month, and next quarter; the queue only stops growing once early cards’ intervals exceed the simulation horizon. Extend the horizon and the plateau moves later and higher.因为间隔会变长，但永远不会停止产生复习。今天加的卡这周会回来、这个月会回来、下个季度还会回来；只有当早期卡的间隔超过模拟跨度，队列才停止增长。把跨度拉长，平台期就会更晚、更高。

Should I trust the minutes-per-day figure?"每天多少分钟"这个数字可信吗？

Only as far as your seconds-per-review input is honest. That number is yours, not ours: time a real session, divide, and enter it. Language cards often run 5–8 seconds, dense cloze cards much longer, and the difference decides whether a plan is comfortable or abandoned.它的可信度完全取决于你填的"每次复习几秒"。这个数只能由你提供：给真实的一次复习计时，除一下再填进来。语言类卡常在 5–8 秒，密集填空卡则长得多，而这个差别决定了计划是舒服还是被放弃。
