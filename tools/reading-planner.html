Reading speed & syllabus planner阅读速度与书单规划器

Most reading plans fail on a guessed speed. Time yourself on two minutes of the actual book, and the arithmetic that follows — days to finish, minutes per day, words per day — becomes worth trusting.多数阅读计划毁在"猜出来的速度"上。就用你真要读的那本书计时两分钟，接下来的算术——多少天读完、每天多少分钟、每天多少词——才值得信任。

How this works它的原理

Measure the material, not the reader要测材料，而不是测读者

Reading rate is a property of the pairing between you and the text. The same person can move three times faster through a novel than a dense textbook, so a single personal "wpm" is close to meaningless for planning.阅读速度是"你与这份文本的配对"的属性。同一个人读小说可能比读硬教材快三倍，所以一个笼统的个人"wpm"对做计划几乎没有意义。

The published averages are lower than folklore公开的平均值比流传的数字低

Brysbaert’s meta-analysis of 190 studies puts adult English silent reading at 238 wpm for non-fiction, with most adults between 175 and 300 — well under the 300–600 wpm often quoted. Recallo therefore measures you instead of assuming a number.Brysbaert 对 190 项研究的元分析给出：成人英语默读非虚构约 238 wpm，多数人落在 175–300 之间——远低于常被引用的 300–600 wpm。因此 Recallo 选择实测你，而不是假设一个数字。

A finish date is a budget, not a wish完成日期是预算，不是愿望

Once rate and volume are real numbers, the plan becomes an honest trade: this many minutes a day, or that many days. Seeing both at once is what stops the familiar pattern of a plan that was arithmetically impossible on day one.当速度和总量都变成真实数字，计划就成了一笔诚实的交易：要么每天这么多分钟，要么总共那么多天。同时看到这两个数，才能避免那种"第一天就在算术上不可能"的老套结局。

Questions常见问题

What is a normal reading speed?正常阅读速度是多少？

For adult English silent reading, the best available synthesis is 238 words per minute for non-fiction and 260 for fiction, with most adults in the 175–300 range and oral reading near 183 wpm. Study reading of dense material commonly runs slower still, which is why measuring beats comparing.就成人英语默读而言，目前最佳综合估计是非虚构约 238 词/分、虚构约 260，多数成人落在 175–300，朗读约 183。研读密集材料通常还要更慢——所以实测胜过对标。

Can I learn to read much faster?能训练到读得快很多吗？

Not without giving something up. Rates far above the normal adult range indicate scanning rather than reading for comprehension; the same meta-analysis found no evidence for distinct "reading gears" beyond that reading/scanning distinction. Scanning is a legitimate tool when finding something is the goal.不可能不付代价。远高于正常成人区间的速度，说明你在扫读，而不是在理解性阅读；同一份元分析未发现除"阅读 vs 扫读"之外还有别的"阅读档位"。当目标是找到某样东西时，扫读是完全正当的工具。

How does this connect to remembering what I read?这和"记住读过的内容"有什么关系？

It does not — finishing a book and retaining it are separate problems. Use this to make the reading plan feasible, then convert what you actually need to keep into cards and schedule those with the FSRS tools. Pages read is not a memory metric.没有关系——读完和记住是两个问题。用本工具让阅读计划可行，然后把真正需要留住的内容转成卡片，用 FSRS 工具排程。"读了多少页"不是记忆指标。
