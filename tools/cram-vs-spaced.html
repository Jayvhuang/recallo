Cram vs spaced comparison临时抱佛脚 vs 间隔复习

Cramming is not lazy — it is a real strategy with a real payoff window, and for a test tomorrow it can win. This holds the number of repetitions constant and shows what each schedule is worth on the day, and a month after.临时抱佛脚不是偷懒——它是有真实收益窗口的策略，对"明天就考"甚至可能更优。本工具固定重复次数不变，展示两种排程分别在考试当天、以及一个月后各值多少。

How this works它的原理

Same repetitions, different spacing, different stability同样的次数，不同的间隔，不同的稳定度

Repetitions packed into one session arrive when recall is still near certain, so each adds little stability. Spread them out and each one lands with recall partly decayed, where a successful retrieval buys much more. The work is identical; the purchase is not.挤在一次里的重复，到来时记得率还接近满值，所以每一次只能加一点稳定度。把它们摊开，每一次都落在"记忆已部分衰减"处，此时一次成功提取能买到多得多。付出的功一样，买到的东西不一样。

Massing wins at very short delays极短延迟下，集中复习会赢

The meta-analytic picture is not "spacing always wins": when the test follows immediately, massed practice can equal or beat spaced practice. The advantage of spacing appears and then grows as the delay lengthens — which is why the two output numbers here matter more than one.元分析给出的图景不是"间隔永远赢"：当测试紧随其后，集中复习可以打平甚至更好。间隔的优势是随着延迟拉长才出现并放大的——这正是本页给出两个数字而非一个的原因。

Choose by when you need it, not by virtue按"你什么时候需要它"来选，而不是按道德感

A vocabulary list for a career, a formula sheet for a test in twelve hours, and a colleague’s name for a meeting are three different decay requirements. Cramming is the correct tool for a short horizon; it is only a mistake when it is used for a long one.为职业生涯背的词表、十二小时后要用的公式表、开会前要记住的同事名字，是三种不同的衰减需求。对短跨度而言，临时抱佛脚就是正确的工具；只有把它用在长跨度上时才是错的。

Questions常见问题

Does cramming actually work?临时抱佛脚真的有用吗？

For an imminent test, yes — massed repetition can match or beat spacing when the delay is very short, because there is no time for the extra stability of spacing to pay off. It fails on the horizon most people actually care about: weeks and months later, where the same repetitions spaced out predict substantially more recall.对马上要考的试，有用——延迟极短时，集中复习可以打平甚至胜过间隔，因为间隔带来的额外稳定度还没有时间兑现。它失效的是大多数人真正在意的跨度：几周、几个月之后，同样次数摊开来的预测记得率会高得多。

How far apart should I space repetitions?重复之间应该隔多远？

Scale the gap to how long you need the memory. Experimental work on the spacing ridgeline finds the best gap grows with the retention interval, so a test next week and a skill for next year do not want the same schedule — and no single gap is optimal for both.按"你需要记多久"来缩放间隔。关于间隔脊线的实验发现，最优间隔随保持期增长；所以"下周考试"和"明年要用的技能"不该用同一套排程，也不存在同时最优的单一间隔。

If I already crammed, is that effort wasted?如果已经临时抱过佛脚，这些努力浪费了吗？

No. Those repetitions built some stability, and the fastest way to keep it is a spaced review before the material decays. Replay your grade history in the FSRS tool to see where you stand, then schedule the next review from there instead of starting over.没有。那些重复建立了一定的稳定度，而保住它最快的方式，是在材料衰减之前安排一次间隔复习。用 FSRS 工具重放你的评分历史，看清目前位置，然后从那里安排下一次，而不是从零重来。
