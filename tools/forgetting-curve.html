Forgetting-curve plotter遗忘曲线绘图器

Ebbinghaus drew this curve in 1885 by memorising nonsense syllables and testing himself. Modern schedulers use a power function of time over stability. Give this one a stability and it tells you the recall probability on any day — and the day any threshold is crossed.1885 年，艾宾浩斯靠背无意义音节并自测，画出了这条曲线。现代排程器用"时间/稳定度"的幂函数来描述它。给它一个稳定度，它就能告诉你任意一天的回忆概率，以及跌破任意阈值的那一天。

How this works它的原理

Forgetting is fast, then slow遗忘先快后慢

The steepest loss happens immediately; the curve then flattens for a long tail. That shape is why one well-timed review beats three reviews crammed into an afternoon — the extra repetitions land where the curve is already flat and buy little.损失最陡的部分发生在最开始，随后曲线拖出长长的平尾。正是这个形状，让"一次时机对的复习"胜过"一个下午挤三次"——多出来的重复落在曲线已经变平的地方，买不到多少东西。

Stability is the horizontal scale稳定度就是横轴的尺度

In R(t,S) = (1 + 19/81 · t/S)^−0.5 the only thing S does is stretch the time axis. Doubling stability does not lift the curve; it doubles how long any given recall level takes to arrive. Successful reviews are how you buy that stretch.在 R(t,S) = (1 + 19/81 · t/S)^−0.5 中，S 唯一的作用是拉伸时间轴。稳定度翻倍不会把曲线抬高，而是让任何一个记得率水平"晚一倍才到达"。成功的复习，就是你买到这份拉伸的方式。

The 1885 curve replicates — with caveats1885 年的曲线可被重复——但有前提

Murre and Dros repeated Ebbinghaus’s procedure in 2015 and recovered a curve close to his original. The caveat matters: those data are nonsense syllables learned by one or two people. The shape generalises; the exact numbers for your meaningful, cued material do not.Murre 与 Dros 在 2015 年重复了艾宾浩斯的程序，得到与原始结果接近的曲线。但前提很重要：那些数据来自一两个人背无意义音节。可推广的是形状，而不是"你那些有意义、有提示的材料"的具体数值。

Questions常见问题

Is the forgetting curve exponential or a power function?遗忘曲线是指数还是幂函数？

Both have been fitted for over a century, and a power function generally describes the long tail better than a single exponential. FSRS uses the power form shown here; the practical consequence is that late forgetting is slower than an exponential predicts, so long intervals are less reckless than they feel.一个多世纪以来两者都被拟合过，而幂函数通常比单一指数更好地描述长尾。FSRS 采用本页展示的幂函数形式；实际后果是：后期遗忘比指数预测得更慢，所以长间隔并不像感觉上那么冒险。

What stability number should I plug in?稳定度该填多少？

Take it from a real card: open the FSRS scheduler tool, replay the grade history of a card you actually have, and use the stability it reports. Typing a round number gives you a shape, not a forecast about your own deck.从一张真实的卡里取：打开 FSRS 排程器工具，重放你手上某张卡的评分历史，用它报出的稳定度。随手填个整数只能得到一个形状，而不是对你自己牌组的预测。

Does the curve apply to skills as well as facts?这条曲线适用于技能吗？

This curve describes retrieval of discrete items. Procedural skills — an instrument, a language’s pronunciation, a sport — decay differently and are usually far more durable. Treat the plot as a model of flashcard-shaped knowledge, not of everything you can do.它描述的是"离散条目的提取"。程序性技能——乐器、语言发音、运动——的衰减方式不同，而且通常耐久得多。请把这张图当作"抽认卡型知识"的模型，而不是你所有能力的模型。
