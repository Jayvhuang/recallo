Focus-block session planner专注块时段规划器

Three hours tonight is not three hours of studying. This lays out the actual clock times — where each block starts, where breaks fall, and what fraction of the evening is focus rather than pause."今晚有三小时"不等于三小时的学习。本工具把真实钟点排出来：每一块什么时候开始、休息落在哪里、以及这个晚上真正专注的比例是多少。

How this works它的原理

Attention habituates to a constant goal注意力会对恒定目标习惯化

Ariga and Lleras showed that performance decline over a long vigilance task is not a drained tank: two brief switches away from the task prevented the decline entirely. Breaks work by deactivating and reactivating the goal, not by refuelling.Ariga 与 Lleras 的实验显示：长时间警觉任务中的表现下降，并非"注意力油箱见底"——只要两次短暂切离任务，下降就被完全阻止。休息起作用的机制是"目标的解除与重新激活"，而不是加油。

The specific numbers are convention, not evidence具体数字是约定，不是证据

The 25/5 pattern is a popular convention. In a head-to-head trial with 94 students it showed no significant advantage over self-timed or flexible breaks on focus or fatigue, and a meta-analysis of 22 micro-break studies found reliable effects on fatigue and vigour but less consistent performance gains. Recallo therefore lets you set the interval instead of prescribing one.25/5 只是流行约定。在一项 94 名学生的直接对比试验中，它在专注与疲劳上未显示出优于自定时或弹性休息的显著优势；一项涵盖 22 项微休息研究的元分析发现，其对疲劳和活力的效果稳定，但对绩效的提升不够一致。因此 Recallo 让你自己设定时长，而不是替你规定。

Clock times beat durations钟点胜过时长

A plan expressed as "50 minutes then a break" quietly loses the transitions. Written as clock times you can see when the session truly ends, and whether the last block is long enough to be worth starting."先 50 分钟再休息"这种写法会悄悄吞掉切换成本。写成钟点，你就能看清这场学习真正几点结束，以及最后那一块是否还长得值得开始。

Questions常见问题

Is the Pomodoro Technique scientifically proven?番茄工作法有科学依据吗？

The general principle — brief breaks prevent the decline that comes from sustained attention to one unchanging goal — has solid experimental support. The specific 25-minute-work, 5-minute-break numbers do not: in a direct comparison with self-timed and flexible breaks they produced no significant advantage. Treat 25/5 as a usable default, not a finding.一般原理有扎实的实验支持——短暂休息能阻止"长时间盯住同一个不变目标"带来的表现下降。但"25 分钟工作 + 5 分钟休息"这组具体数字没有：在与自定时、弹性休息的直接对比中，它没有显示出显著优势。请把 25/5 当作可用的默认值，而不是研究结论。

How long should my blocks be?一块应该多长？

Long enough to get into the work, short enough that you notice when attention drifts. For rote or repetitive work, shorter fixed blocks are a reasonable default; for demanding analytical work, longer blocks with breaks taken when focus actually slips fit the evidence better than a fixed timer.长到足以进入工作状态，短到你还能察觉注意力开始飘。机械、重复的工作，用较短的固定块是合理默认；高强度分析性工作，则"块更长、在专注真的下滑时才休息"比固定计时器更符合证据。

What counts as a real break?什么才算真正的休息？

Something that genuinely deactivates the task goal — standing up, walking, looking out of a window. Scrolling a feed keeps you in a demanding visual-verbal task, so it is closer to switching work than to resting. The planner schedules the slot; what you put in it decides whether it works.能真正"解除任务目标"的事情——站起来、走一走、看看窗外。刷信息流让你仍处在高负荷的视觉—语言任务里，更接近换一份工作而不是休息。规划器只负责排出这个时段；放什么进去，决定它是否有效。
