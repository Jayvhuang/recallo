FSRS interval schedulerFSRS 间隔排程器

Grade a card the way you would in Anki and watch the memory model respond: stability, difficulty, recall probability at review time, and the interval that follows. This is the FSRS-5 algorithm, implemented from the published equations and default weights.像在 Anki 里那样给卡片评分，实时看记忆模型如何反应：稳定度、难度、复习时刻的回忆概率，以及随之而来的间隔。这里跑的是 FSRS-5 算法，依据公开方程与默认权重实现。

How this works它的原理

Three numbers describe one card三个数字描述一张卡

FSRS keeps stability S (how many days until recall falls to 90%), difficulty D on a 1–10 scale, and retrievability R (the probability you can recall it right now). Every grade you press updates S and D; R is then a pure function of elapsed time and S.FSRS 为每张卡维护三个量：稳定度 S（多少天后记得率降到 90%）、难度 D（1–10）、可提取性 R（此刻还能回忆起来的概率）。你每次评分都会更新 S 与 D；而 R 只是"经过时间"和 S 的函数。

The interval is solved, not guessed间隔是解出来的，不是猜的

Recall decays as R(t,S) = (1 + 19/81 · t/S)^−0.5. Set R to your target and solve for t: that is the next interval. Raising the target from 0.90 to 0.97 does not add a little work — it divides every interval, which is exactly what the numbers here let you see.记忆按 R(t,S) = (1 + 19/81 · t/S)^−0.5 衰减。把 R 设为你的目标再反解 t，就是下一次间隔。把目标从 0.90 提到 0.97 不是"多一点工作量"——它会成倍压缩每一个间隔，本页的数字正是让你亲眼看到这一点。

A lapse is not a reset遗忘不等于清零

On Again, SM-2 sends the interval back to one day. FSRS instead computes a post-lapse stability from the stability you had already built, so a mature card that slips does not return to zero. Press Good four times, then Again, and compare.按 重来 时，SM-2 会把间隔打回 1 天。FSRS 则依据你已累积的稳定度计算"遗忘后稳定度"，所以一张成熟卡失手一次不会回到原点。试试连按四次"良好"再按一次"重来"，对比看看。

Questions常见问题

Does this match what Anki will schedule?这里和 Anki 排的一样吗？

The equations and default weights are the published FSRS-5 ones, so a card with the same grade history and the same desired retention lands on the same interval. Two things will make your app differ: if you (or its optimiser) have trained custom parameters on your own review history, and Anki’s fuzz, which shifts due dates by a small random amount to spread the queue.方程与默认权重都取自公开的 FSRS-5，因此在相同评分历史、相同目标记得率下，间隔是一致的。两种情况会造成差异：一是你（或优化器）用自己的复习历史训练了自定义参数；二是 Anki 的 fuzz 会给到期日加一点随机抖动，把队列摊开。

What should I press when I barely remembered it?想了很久才想起来，该按哪个？

Hard, not Again. Again means you failed to retrieve it, which triggers the lapse path and rebuilds stability from a lower base. Hard means you got there slowly. Mixing the two up is the single most common reason a real deck behaves differently from any calculator.按"困难"，不要按"重来"。"重来"表示你没能回忆出来，会走遗忘路径、从更低的基线重建稳定度；"困难"表示你想起来了，只是慢。混用这两个键，是真实牌组表现与任何计算器不符的最常见原因。

Why is the first interval so short?为什么第一个间隔这么短？

A brand-new card has no accumulated stability — initial stability comes straight from the parameter set for the grade you pressed. Stability grows fastest in the first few successful reviews, which is why intervals stretch quickly at the start and then settle into a smoother multiplier.全新卡没有任何累积稳定度——初始稳定度直接由你按下的评分对应的参数决定。稳定度在最初几次成功复习中增长最快，所以开头间隔拉伸得很猛，之后才进入较平缓的倍增。
