SM-2 interval calculatorSM-2 间隔计算器

SM-2 has been scheduling flashcards since 1987 and still powers many apps. It is simple enough to follow by hand: one ease factor per card, multiply, and reset to one day on a lapse. Stepping through it is the clearest way to see what FSRS changed.SM-2 从 1987 年就在为抽认卡排程，至今仍是许多应用的内核。它足够简单，可以手算跟下来：每张卡一个易度因子，乘上去，一旦遗忘就打回 1 天。逐步走一遍，是理解"FSRS 到底改了什么"最清楚的方式。

How this works它的原理

One number per card每张卡只有一个数

SM-2 stores an E-Factor, starting at 2.5 and moving with your grades, bounded below at 1.3. The interval sequence is fixed at the start — 1 day, then 6 days — and after that each interval is the previous one times the E-Factor.SM-2 为每张卡存一个易度因子 E-Factor：初值 2.5，随评分变动，下界 1.3。开头的间隔序列是固定的——1 天、6 天——之后每个间隔都是"上一个间隔 × E-Factor"。

A lapse costs everything一次遗忘，代价是全部

Fail a card and SM-2 sends the interval back to the beginning, no matter how many months it had survived. This is the behaviour FSRS replaces with post-lapse stability, and it is the single biggest practical difference between the two.一旦答错，SM-2 就把间隔打回起点——不管这张卡此前已经存活了多少个月。FSRS 用"遗忘后稳定度"取代了这个行为，这也是两者最大的实际差别。

Simplicity is a feature and a limit简单既是优点也是局限

You can audit SM-2 on paper, which is why it is a good teaching model and easy to trust. What it cannot do is separate "how hard this card is" from "how well it is currently learned" — one number carries both, so it over-schedules mature cards and under-schedules fragile ones.SM-2 可以在纸上审计，所以它是很好的教学模型，也容易信任。它做不到的是把"这张卡有多难"和"当前学得多牢"分开——一个数承担了两件事，于是成熟卡被过度排程，脆弱卡被排得不足。

Questions常见问题

Should I use SM-2 or FSRS?该用 SM-2 还是 FSRS？

If your app offers FSRS, it generally schedules more efficiently, mainly because a lapse no longer discards accumulated stability and intervals are solved for an explicit retention target. SM-2 remains a sound, auditable default and is far better than no scheduling at all.如果你的应用提供 FSRS，它通常排得更高效，主要因为遗忘不再丢弃已累积的稳定度，且间隔是按显式的记得率目标解出来的。但 SM-2 仍是稳妥、可审计的默认选择，远胜于完全不做排程。

Why 1 day and then 6 days?为什么是 1 天然后 6 天？

Those two values are specified constants in SM-2, not derived quantities: I(1) = 1 and I(2) = 6, with multiplication by the E-Factor only from the third repetition. Many apps expose them as editable "learning steps", which is why your app may differ.这两个值是 SM-2 里规定的常数，不是推导出来的：I(1) = 1、I(2) = 6，从第三次重复起才乘 E-Factor。许多应用把它们做成可编辑的"学习步骤"，所以你的应用可能不同。

What does the 1.3 floor on ease do?易度下界 1.3 有什么用？

It stops a repeatedly failed card from collapsing to a permanent one-day loop. In practice, a card that has hit the floor is telling you the card is broken, not the schedule: split it, add a cue, or rewrite it rather than grinding it daily.它防止一张反复答错的卡塌缩成永久的"每天一次"死循环。实践中，撞到下界的卡是在告诉你"卡本身有问题"，而不是排程有问题：把它拆开、加提示、或者重写，而不是每天硬磨。
