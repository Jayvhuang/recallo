The principle underneath底层原理

Three ideas do all the work in every tool on this site.整站工具都建立在三个想法之上。

1 · Forgetting is a curve you can price1 · 遗忘是一条可以定价的曲线

FSRS models recall probability as R(t,S) = (1 + 19/81 · t/S)^−0.5. Give it the memory stability of a card and it tells you the day recall falls to any level — so "when should I review?" becomes arithmetic instead of a feeling. FSRS 把回忆概率建模为 R(t,S) = (1 + 19/81 · t/S)^−0.5。给出一张卡的记忆稳定度，它就能算出记得率跌到任意水平的那一天——于是"什么时候该复习"从感觉变成了算术。

2 · Every new card is a subscription2 · 每张新卡都是一份订阅

A card you add today returns roughly seven to nine times in its first year. Twenty new cards a day is therefore not twenty cards of work — it is a queue that keeps growing for months before it flattens. Simulating it is the only honest way to see the bill. 今天加的一张卡，第一年大约会回来七到九次。所以每天 20 张新卡不是 20 张的工作量——那是一条会持续增长数月才趋平的队列。只有模拟才能看到真实账单。

3 · Difficulty in the moment is not waste3 · 当下的吃力不是浪费

Spacing and interleaving both feel worse while you do them and usually test better later. The tools here quantify that gap instead of asking you to take it on faith. 间隔与交错在练的时候都更难受，但后测通常更好。这里的工具把这个差距量化出来，而不是让你凭信念接受。

Questions people actually ask常见问题

How many new cards a day can I actually sustain?我每天到底能承受多少张新卡？

Fewer than you think, because the cost is the review queue rather than the new cards. At 20 new cards a day with default timings, the workload simulator settles well above 20 reviews a day within a couple of months. Run it with your own answer speed before choosing a number. 比你想的少，因为代价在复习队列而不在新卡本身。按默认作答时间、每天 20 张新卡，负荷模拟器在两个月内就会稳定在远高于每天 20 次复习的水平。用你自己的作答速度先跑一遍再定数字。

What should desired retention be?目标记得率该设多少？

Anki's FSRS default is 0.90. Because interval length falls steeply as the target rises, 0.97 can cost several times the daily minutes of 0.85 while adding comparatively few remembered cards. The retention optimiser ranks each setting by cards recallable per daily study minute — then override it deliberately if a single failure is expensive. Anki 的 FSRS 默认是 0.90。由于目标越高间隔缩短得越猛，0.97 每天要花的时间可能是 0.85 的数倍，多记住的卡却相对有限。优化器按"每天每分钟换得多少可回忆卡"排序——但如果记错一项代价很高，就该主动选更高的值。

Is Recallo a flashcard app?Recallo 是抽认卡应用吗？

No. It has no cards and no review sessions. It is the calculator layer that flashcard apps do not give you: what a setting costs, whether a plan fits, and what the model predicts. Keep using Anki, Mnemosyne, RemNote or paper. 不是。它没有卡片，也没有复习会话。它提供的是抽认卡应用不会给你的那一层：某个设置要付多少代价、某个计划装不装得下、模型如何预测。你继续用 Anki、Mnemosyne、RemNote 或纸卡都行。

Where do the numbers come from?这些数字从哪来？

From the published algorithms, run locally — not from a private dataset and not from any claim about "average users". FSRS-5 uses its published default weights; SM-2 follows Woźniak's published description; simulations are seeded so the same inputs always give identical output. Sources are listed below and on every tool page. 来自公开算法在本地的运算——不来自任何私有数据集，也不来自关于"平均用户"的说法。FSRS-5 使用其公开默认参数，SM-2 遵循 Woźniak 公开的算法描述，模拟使用固定随机种子，因此同样输入永远得到同样输出。来源列在下方及每个工具页。

Are these predictions about me personally?这些预测适用于我个人吗？

Only as far as the model and your inputs are right. FSRS default weights describe an aggregate of many users' review logs, not your memory; your own answer times and grade mix matter more than any decimal place here. Treat outputs as planning estimates, not measurements of your brain. 只在模型与你的输入都成立的范围内适用。FSRS 默认参数描述的是大量用户复习日志的整体规律，而不是你的记忆；你自己的作答时间与评分构成，比这里的小数位更重要。请把输出当作规划估算，而不是对你大脑的测量。
