Privacy隐私政策

Our default position is privacy-first: the calculators run entirely in your browser and the numbers you enter never leave your device. 我们的默认立场是隐私优先：计算器完全在你的浏览器中运行，你输入的数字从不离开本设备。

What we store on your device我们存在你设备上的内容

Recallo saves only your language preference in your browser’s localStorage (not a cookie). You can clear it any time from your browser settings. No study inputs, results, or calculations are transmitted anywhere. Recallo 仅把你选择的语言偏好保存在浏览器的 localStorage（不是 cookie）中。你可随时在浏览器设置里清除。任何学习输入、结果或计算都不会被传送到任何地方。

Server logs & IP address服务器日志与 IP 地址

Like almost every website, our hosting provider records standard server logs when you visit — including your IP address, the page requested, browser type, and time. These logs are used for security and operation and are not sold or shared for advertising. 像几乎所有网站一样，我们的托管服务商在你访问时会记录标准服务器日志——包括你的 IP 地址、请求的页面、浏览器类型和时间。这些日志用于安全和运维，不会被出售或用于广告。

Cookies & web beaconsCookie 与网络信标

In its default state this site sets no advertising cookies and loads no web beacons (invisible tracking pixels). If we later enable Google AdSense Auto Ads, Google may set advertising cookies and may use a web beacon to measure ad delivery, under Google’s advertising policies. We will not run ad scripts on this privacy page. 在默认状态下，本网站不设置任何广告 cookie，也不加载网络信标（不可见追踪像素）。若我们日后启用 Google AdSense 自动广告，Google 可能会设置广告 cookie，并可能使用网络信标来衡量广告投放，遵循 Google 的广告政策。我们不会在本隐私页面运行广告脚本。

Google advertising & partnersGoogle 广告与合作伙伴

When AdSense is enabled, Google and its partners may serve ads based on your visit to this and other sites. You can manage personalised advertising through your Google Ads settings and learn how Google uses data from sites you visit here: 启用 AdSense 后，Google 及其合作伙伴可能根据你的访问在本站及其他网站投放广告。你可通过 Google 广告设置管理个性化广告，并在此处了解 Google 如何使用你访问网站的数据：policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
